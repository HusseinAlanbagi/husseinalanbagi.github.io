# TeXbench: licences and credits

TeXbench is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from Overleaf
or any other LaTeX service, and it contains none of their code, templates, text or artwork.

The code written for TeXbench itself (the `core.js` sources, `app.css`, everything in `modules/`,
and the changes made to the equation editor and IRF plotter) belongs to Hussein Alanbagi.

## Names

LaTeX, TeX Live, Dynare, Overleaf, Word and other names mentioned in the app belong to their owners
and are used only to say what the app works with (for example "import a project .zip").
