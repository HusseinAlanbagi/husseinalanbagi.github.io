Research

Oil, fiscal policy and central banking in Iraq

My PhD builds dynamic stochastic general equilibrium (DSGE) models of Iraq's economy to study oil-price shocks, government spending, monetary policy and fiscal dominance.

How Oil Price and Government Spending Shocks Affect Macroeconomic Dynamics in Iraq? A DSGE Approach

Al-Anbagi, H. · First PhD essay · 2026

A DSGE model of Iraq that traces how shocks to oil prices and to government spending pass through the economy, and how the interaction between fiscal and monetary policy shapes that response.

  • Presented at the School of Economics PGR Conference, University of Sheffield, May 2026
